PAICONS Self-Development Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Developer: PAICONS (paicons.com) · Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]

In short: PAICONS Self-Development counts how often you move to a new short video in Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels and TikTok. It does this on your phone only. Your activity is never uploaded, sold or shared, and it is deleted automatically after 30 days. The only thing that leaves your phone is Pro subscription verification, and only if you buy Pro.

1. Accessibility service

Automatic counting uses Android's AccessibilityService API. It runs only after you agree to the in-app disclosure and turn the service on in Android settings. While it is on:

  • Android delivers events to it only from Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and TikTok. Events from all other apps are ignored.
  • Android may make on-screen interface information from those apps available to the service, which can include text. PAICONS Self-Development uses only the position, size and scroll movement of scrolling areas to detect a move to a new video. All other information is discarded immediately and never stored.
  • PAICONS Self-Development never reads or stores captions, comments, messages, creator names, video IDs, links, screenshots or recordings. It never taps, scrolls, blocks apps or changes settings.
  • You can turn the service off at any time in Android settings, or withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy & permissions.

3. Optional permissions

  • Usage access (optional): shows total time spent in the four supported apps, read from Android's usage statistics on your device. It is not uploaded.
  • Notifications (optional): sends goal reminders, generated locally.
  • Display over other apps (optional): shows the floating reminder bubble.

4. Pro subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Google Play. PAICONS Self-Development never receives your payment details. When you buy or restore Pro, the app sends the Google Play purchase token, product ID and app package name over HTTPS to our verification server. The server checks the purchase with Google Play's Developer API.

  • The server stores only a one-way hash of the purchase token, plus the product, subscription status and expiry date. These are used to unlock Pro and to handle renewals, cancellations and refunds.
  • The server hosting provider (Google Cloud) may record your IP address in standard request logs for security and operations. These logs are retained for a limited period under the provider's default settings.
  • No PAICONS Self-Development activity is ever sent to the server.

Google Play and the Google Play Billing library process purchase data under Google's Privacy Policy.

5. What we don't do

  • The paicons.com download (APK) contains no billing or network features other than links you choose to open.
  • No accounts, sign-in, advertising, advertising IDs or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • Your data is not used for advertising or profiling.

6. Children

PAICONS Self-Development is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Your choices and rights

You can stop tracking, delete all activity, withdraw consent or uninstall at any time. Because activity data never leaves your device, we cannot access or recover it. For questions about subscription records on our server, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL].

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. If the change is material, we will also update the in-app disclosure.

9. Not affiliated

PAICONS Self-Development is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Meta, Instagram, Facebook, Google, YouTube or TikTok.
