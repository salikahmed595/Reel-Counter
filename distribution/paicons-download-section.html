Install steps
  1. Tap Get it on Google Play. Testers: open the testing link you received and tap Become a tester.
  2. Tap Install, then Open.
  3. Read the privacy screen and tap Agree.
  4. Follow the numbered steps to turn on PAICONS Self-Development tracking.
  5. Pick a daily limit. You're done. The app shows a short checklist for anything left.

Why Google Play? Android blocks apps that use the Accessibility service when they're installed from a website or chat app. Google Play installs are reviewed and safe.
